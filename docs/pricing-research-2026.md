# Pricing research (October 2026)

Market rates for Dhia's three services: brand design, web development and
training. **64 sources**: 39 international, 25 Tunisian. Amounts in TND use
3.4 TND = 1 EUR.

**How this was gathered:** the research ran from a cloud session whose network
blocked opening the pages themselves. Every figure comes from search-engine
extracts of the cited page. The URLs are real search results, but **open the
page and check a figure before quoting it publicly**. Figures marked *weak* are
anecdotal, could not be tied to a publisher, or come from a vendor with an
interest in the number.

## 1. Recommendation: "from" prices for the site

Dhia's position:
- **Experience:** about 3–5 years.
- **Languages:** trilingual (AR/FR/EN), including Arabic right-to-left work.
- **Certification:** CNFCPP-certified trainer.
- **Stack:** custom Next.js sites rather than WordPress templates.
- **Clients:** Tunisian SMEs and NGOs, plus remote international clients.

That puts him at the **upper-middle of the Tunisian market** and the
**lower-middle of the international one**.

### Training and facilitation

| Format | Tunisian companies | Tunisian / MENA NGOs and donors | International (EU, UN, INGO) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Training day (6 h) | **600–650 TND** | **500–600 TND** | **€200–300 / $250–350 + travel and per diem** | The CNFCPP reimburses a company up to 20% of the SMIG per training hour: about **111 TND/h, or about 666 TND per 6-hour day in 2026** (#T17–T18). Staying under that cap means a company client gets 100% back, which is a strong selling point. CONECT's donor-funded programmes paid about 500–667 TND/day in 2025 (#T19–T20). The Council of Europe pays junior trainers €160 and senior trainers €300 per day (#X3). Mid-level local consultants in MENA earn $250–300 per day (#X11). |
| Half-day workshop | from 350 TND | from 300 TND | from €150 | No Tunisian source quotes a half-day rate. 55–60% of a day rate is the usual convention (#X17–X18). |
| Train-the-trainer (2–5 days) | 650 TND/day + prep | 550–600 TND/day + prep | €250–300/day | Senior rates apply to TOT work. |
| Preparation and design days | same day rate, pro rata | same | same | The UN, GIZ and EUAA all count preparation and reporting as paid expert-days (#X1, #X6, #X9). SALTO pays a lump sum for them (#X5). |
| Keynote (45–90 min) | from 400 TND | often an honorarium | from €300 | Treat a keynote as a half-day. The international entry level is $1,500+, but that is US-centric (#X18). |
| Online session | same as in person | same | same | UK and US rate cards price virtual sessions about the same as in person (#X17–X18). |

**What to show on the site.** Show "from" prices for the design and web
packages: Tunisian buyers compare agency price lists, and every agency in #T1–T16
publishes its own.

For training, show "**day rate from 500 TND**" with the line "*for companies,
eligible for TFP reimbursement through the CNFCPP*". Organisers also expect a
custom quote, so the FAQ's quote answer stays valid.

**Check before using the reimbursement line:** confirm with the CNFCPP that
training Dhia delivers qualifies. The company files the claim, and Dhia may need
to be registered as a training provider.

**Do not undersell.** The Fiverr and Upwork floors (logo $30–150, $10–15/h for
Tunisian designers) are the commodity market. Showing trilingual work, case
studies and a certification moves Dhia out of it.
