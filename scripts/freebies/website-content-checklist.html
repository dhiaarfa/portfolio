Outgrown your Facebook page? The Website Content Checklist

Everything to prepare before you hire anyone to build your website, so the project takes weeks instead of months. Includes the extra steps for Arabic, French and English sites.

By Mohamed Dhia Arfa · dhia-portfolio.com

Signs you've outgrown a Facebook page

  • Customers ask the same questions in Messenger every day
  • People search your name on Google and find nothing you control
  • You can't show your full catalogue, menu or services in one place
  • Partners or foreign clients ask for "your website"
  • You depend on one platform's algorithm to be seen
  • You can't take bookings, quotes or orders without chatting

1. Goals

  • The one action a visitor should take (call, book, order, request a quote)
  • Who the site is for (customers, partners, recruiters, funders)
  • 3 websites you like, and why
  • How you will measure success after 3 months

2. Content

  • One-sentence description of what you do
  • List of services or products, with prices or "from" prices if you show them
  • About text: who you are, since when, where
  • 3-6 customer reviews (with permission)
  • Answers to the 5 questions customers ask most
  • Contact details, opening hours, map location

3. Visuals

  • Logo files (vector or high-resolution PNG)
  • Brand colours as HEX codes, fonts
  • Real photos: team, place, products (phone photos in daylight beat stock)
  • Photo permissions from people who appear in them

4. Languages

  • Which languages, and which one is the default
  • Texts written by a native speaker for each language, not machine-translated
  • Arabic pages checked right-to-left (menus, forms, icons)
  • Arabic and Latin fonts chosen as a pair

5. Accounts & access

  • Domain name registered in your name (.tn or .com)
  • Hosting account owned by you, not only by the developer
  • Google Business Profile claimed
  • Social media links and WhatsApp number

6. Launch

  • Tested on a mid-range phone with mobile data, not just office Wi-Fi
  • Page titles and descriptions written for Google
  • Contact form tested end to end
  • Analytics installed
  • You know how to update texts and photos yourself
The fastest projects start with content. Design waits for text and photos; prepare them first and the build itself can take two to four weeks.
