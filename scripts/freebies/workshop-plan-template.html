1. Workshop overview

2. Why this workshop?

If an objective can't be observed or measured at the end of the day, rewrite it. "Understand leadership" can't be checked; "plan a 30-minute team meeting with a clear agenda" can.

3. Session flow

Time every block. Plan an energizer after any 45-60 minutes of sitting, and keep 10% of the total time as buffer.

Debrief questions that work

  • What happened? What did you notice?
  • So what? What does it tell you about how you work, decide, or lead?
  • Now what? What will you do differently next week?

4. Logistics & risks

Materials

  • Flipchart + paper
  • Markers (3 colours)
  • Sticky notes
  • Tape / blu-tack
  • Name tags
  • Printed handouts (+10%)
  • Projector, cables, clicker
  • Attendance sheet

Room layout

  • Circle (discussion, trust)
  • Islands of 4-6 (group work)
  • U-shape (presentations)
  • Open space (energizers, simulations)
RiskPlan B
Running late / fewer hours than planned
Low energy after lunch
No projector / no internet
Mixed-language group
Fewer or more participants than expected
