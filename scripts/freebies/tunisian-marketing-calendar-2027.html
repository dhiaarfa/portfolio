Tunisian Marketing Calendar 2027

Every date and season that moves attention in Tunisia, month by month, with a content idea for each. Plan your posts, packaging runs and campaigns before the rush, not during it.

By Mohamed Dhia Arfa · Zia Studio · dhia-portfolio.com
Islamic holidays follow the lunar calendar. Dates marked "around" are astronomical estimates and can shift by a day with the official moon sighting. Confirm them with the official announcement before booking print or ads.

January Reset

  • 1 Jan New Year's Day
  • Olive oil season in full swing (harvest Nov-Jan)
Idea: "Our year in 9 posts" carousel; behind-the-scenes of production.

February Ramadan prep

  • 14 Feb Valentine's Day
  • Around 8 Feb Ramadan begins
  • Final two weeks of January: prepare Ramadan visuals and packaging
Idea: Ramadan template set (iftar times, offers, opening hours) ready before day one.

March Eid & national pride

  • Around 9-10 Mar Eid al-Fitr
  • 20 Mar Independence Day
Idea: Eid greeting in Arabic and French with your brand colours, not a generic template; gift boxes for food brands.

April Spring

  • 9 Apr Martyrs' Day
  • Spring events, conferences and student clubs season
Idea: Event sponsorship visuals; customer stories while traffic is calm.

May Eid al-Adha

  • 1 May Labour Day
  • Around 16-17 May Eid al-Adha
  • Exam season starts for students
Idea: Exam-season tips (education, cafés with study spaces); Eid opening-hours posts.

June Summer starts

  • Around 6 Jun Islamic New Year
  • Baccalaureate results; tourist season opens
Idea: Summer menu or collection launch; congratulation posts for graduates.

July Peak season

  • 25 Jul Republic Day
  • Tunisians living abroad return home (July-August)
  • Coastal tourism and festival season
Idea: Content in French and Arabic for returning families; travel and gift products.

August Women & summer

  • 13 Aug Women's Day (Fête de la Femme)
  • Around 14 Aug Prophet's Birthday (Mouled)
Idea: Spotlight the women behind your business; Mouled treats for food brands.

September Rentrée

  • Back to school (mid-September)
  • Businesses restart after summer: best month for B2B outreach
Idea: Back-to-school offers; "new season, new look" rebrand reveals.

October Harvest & trade

  • 15 Oct Evacuation Day
  • Date harvest begins (Deglet Nour, Oct-Dec)
  • Trade fairs and export season for food brands
Idea: Harvest stories and packaging reveals for dates and olive oil brands.

November Sales

  • 26 Nov Black Friday
  • Olive harvest starts
Idea: Plan offers 3 weeks ahead; prepare story templates for a 4-day countdown.

December Year end

  • 17 Dec Revolution Day
  • End-of-year gifts, corporate gift boxes, New Year's Eve
Idea: Corporate gift catalogue; a "thank you" post naming real customers (with permission).

How to use this calendar

  • Mark the 6-8 dates that matter to your customers; ignore the rest.
  • Prepare visuals three weeks before each date, print four to six weeks before.
  • Build one reusable template per moment (Ramadan, Eid, summer, sales) instead of designing from scratch each year.
  • Publish in the languages your customers read: Arabic, French, or both.
  • During Ramadan, attention shifts to the hours before iftar and late evening: schedule posts accordingly.
  • Keep every post on-brand: same colours, same type, same logo file. Seasonal does not mean off-brand.
