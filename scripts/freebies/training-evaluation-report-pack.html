Training Evaluation & Report Pack

Three templates that turn a workshop into results you can report: a pre/post self-assessment, a participant feedback form, and a donor-ready training report.

By Mohamed Dhia Arfa · CNFCPP-certified trainer · dhia-portfolio.com

How the pack works

  1. Before the first activity, participants fill in Part A (5 minutes).
  2. At the end, they fill in the same Part A again, then Part B.
  3. Within a week, you complete Part C with the averages and send it to the organiser or funder.
Write the 5 statements in Part A from your learning objectives. "I can plan a 30-minute team meeting with a clear agenda" is measurable; "I understand leadership" is not.
