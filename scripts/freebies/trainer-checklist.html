The 30-Point Pre-Training Checklist

Run through it before every workshop. Most delivery problems are logistics problems you could have caught the day before.

By Mohamed Dhia Arfa · CNFCPP-certified trainer · dhia-portfolio.com

One week before

  • Objectives agreed with the organiser, in writing
  • Participant list: number, age range, languages, needs
  • Needs analysis done (what they know, what must change)
  • Session plan timed block by block, with buffer
  • Pre-assessment ready (3-5 questions or a quick poll)
  • Venue confirmed: room size, layout, access, Wi-Fi
  • Accessibility checked (stairs, seating, hearing, prayer times)
  • Co-facilitator roles split and rehearsed

Two days before

  • Slides final, exported to PDF as a backup
  • Handouts printed (+10% spares)
  • Materials packed: markers, flipchart, sticky notes, tape
  • Energizers chosen for each low-energy moment
  • Plan B for each activity if time runs short
  • Reminder message sent to participants (time, place, what to bring)

On the day, before people arrive

  • Arrive 45 minutes early
  • Room set in the layout the activities need (circle, islands, U)
  • Projector, sound, clicker and extension cable tested
  • Wi-Fi and any online tool (poll, form) tested on a phone
  • Agenda and objectives visible on a flipchart
  • Attendance sheet and name tags ready
  • Water, coffee break and lunch timings confirmed
  • Phone on silent, timer ready

During the session

  • Group agreements set in the first 15 minutes
  • Language check: who is more comfortable in Arabic, French, English?
  • Energy check every 45-60 minutes
  • Parking lot for off-topic questions

Right after

  • Post-assessment and feedback form completed before people leave
  • Photos of flipcharts and outputs taken
  • Attendance and results saved for the report
  • Follow-up message with resources sent within 48 hours
The pre/post pair is what makes results reportable. Ask the same 3-5 questions at the start and at the end: the difference is the evidence funders and organisers ask for.
